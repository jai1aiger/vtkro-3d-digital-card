// Module 01: Client Intelligence

Targeting Strategy: Big Businesses vs. Small Businesses

Different business sizes have completely opposite buying motivations. Match your pitch to their core psychology.

1. Big Businesses (Hospitals, Malls, Luxury Cafes, Supermarkets)

  • Core Priority: QUALITY, REPUTATION & MULTIPLE BUNDLED SERVICES.
  • Buyer Psychology: Large enterprises do NOT care about saving ₹5,000. They fear poor aesthetics, server crashes, and brand embarrassment.
  • Pitch Angle: Focus on end-to-end digital authority: multi-page web presence, secure payment gateways, staff digital visiting cards, and automated CRM workflows.

2. Small Businesses (Local Salons, Bakeries, Neighborhood Clinics)

  • Core Priority: COST-EFFICIENCY & IMMEDIATE ROI.
  • Buyer Psychology: Small shop owners dread recurring monthly agency bills and complex tech they don't understand.
  • Pitch Angle: Emphasize transparent, one-time pricing, zero maintenance headaches, and instant WhatsApp inquiry generation.

SANJAI KUMAR'S CARDINAL RULE:

Never pitch "low-cost budget" to a premier hospital, and never overwhelm a neighborhood bakery with complex corporate stack jargon!

Bigli's Value Scale

Enterprise demands Quality & Multi-Services. Local retail demands Cost-Efficiency & Instant Return.

Trainer Notes

Coach agents to perform a 60-second qualification before dialing: Is this business owner motivated by prestige or cost savings? Leading with the wrong value proposition causes immediate friction.

// Module 02: Outreach Protocol

How to Approach a Client: The "Call First" Golden Rule

Why phone briefings protect your authority and eliminate embarrassing walk-in rejections.

✓ The Mandatory Phone Briefing (ALWAYS DO THIS)

  • Respects Their Schedule: Owners are occupied with daily logistics, customers, and inventory.
  • Qualifies the Lead: A quick 2-minute briefing sets up professional expectations before you invest travel time.
  • 80%+ Higher Show-Up Rate: A pre-booked appointment ensures a dedicated sit-down where they are attentive.

✗ CRITICAL AVOID: Never Walk In Unannounced!

  • Instant Defensive Rejection: An unannounced walk-in catches them off-guard, triggering a quick "Not interested, come back later".
  • Lowers Professional Status: Unscheduled visits make you look like a desperate salesperson rather than a VTKRO tech consultant.

THE NEIGHBORHOOD RESIDENCY RULE:

Even if the prospective client's business is 50 meters away from your home, CALL FIRST. Professionalism always beats convenience.

Bigli's Appointment Rule

Confirm the meeting slot on the phone before stepping foot through their doors!

Trainer Notes

Remind agents that cold walk-ins burn out beginners quickly. Calling first shields them from physical fatigue and puts them in control of their daily calendar.

The Cold Call Opening: The 2-Minute Hook

Command attention, establish credibility, and win listening permission in under 120 seconds.

Official Cold Call Script

"Hello [Client's Name], good morning/afternoon!

My name is [Agent Name] calling on behalf of VTKRO, a premier web development and digital solutions company.

I’m reaching out because we help businesses like [Client's Business Name] elevate their online presence, attract more customers, and automate their operations. We are currently offering specialized business growth website packages tailored to your exact industry needs.

◈ 3 Delivery Secrets of Elite Telecallers:

  • Smile Through the Receiver: Physical smiling elevates vocal pitch and projects warmth.
  • Name Precision: State their exact business name so it sounds like bespoke research, not a robot blast.
  • The 2-Minute Micro-Agreement: Asking for only two minutes lowers resistance immediately.
Bigli's 2-Minute Telecall

High energy, crystal-clear pacing, and a crisp 120-second timer to win their attention.

Trainer Notes

Have agents practice pausing right after asking: "Do you have two minutes...?" The first person to speak loses. Wait patiently for their 'Yes' or 'Sure, what is it?'.

// Module 11: Closing Formula

The Power Close: Alternative Choice & Demo Booking

Eliminate hesitation by replacing open-ended questions with structured alternative choices.

Official Closing Script

"Based on your current operations at [Client's Business Name], would you prefer starting with our ₹15,000 Standard Site, upgrading to the ₹25,000 Payment & Branding Suite, or leveraging the ₹50,000 E-Commerce & WhatsApp Chatbot Setup?

I can reserve a slot for our senior technical strategist to run a quick 10-minute demo for you this afternoon. Would 3:00 PM or 5:00 PM work better?"

◈ Psychological Power of the Alternative Close:

  • Never Ask Yes/No: Asking "Do you want to buy?" invites an easy "No".
  • Choice of Winners: Offering Package A vs B vs C forces their mind to choose an option, not whether to buy.
  • The 3 PM vs 5 PM Lock: By offering two specific times, their brain considers their schedule rather than rejecting the meeting.
  • The 10-Minute Low Commitment: 10 minutes feels risk-free for a busy business owner.
Bigli's 10-Minute Demo Lock

Offer "3:00 PM or 5:00 PM?" to secure the demonstration and seal the deal effortlessly.

Trainer Notes

Remember: The agent's mission on the phone is NOT to close the ₹50,000 deal; it is to CLOSE THE 10-MINUTE DEMO! Once the senior technical strategist delivers the demo, our closing rate exceeds 60%.
